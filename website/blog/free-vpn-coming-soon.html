A Free VPN Is Coming to Free Disk Analyzer

2026 · 4 min read

A built-in VPN has been on the roadmap for a while, and it's now far enough along to talk about publicly. This post is deliberately short on promises and long on what's actually true right now.

What's decided

  • It will be free, alongside the free app, no VPN behind a required paywall.
  • It's being built on real infrastructure (WireGuard, via an infrastructure partner), not a placeholder.
  • Planned server locations include the US, UK, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Switzerland, Japan, Singapore, and Canada, the final list depends on partner availability.

What's not decided yet

How this gets funded, exactly, is still open. Free VPN infrastructure isn't free to run, so some model has to cover that: possibly short ads for extra usage, possibly an optional paid tier for people who'd rather skip ads entirely, possibly something else. Nothing here is final, and nothing will be sold or gated until the VPN itself actually works. A "coming soon" page that already charges for the thing it's teasing isn't something this project is going to do.

In the meantime

If you need a VPN today, NordVPN is a real, working option (affiliate link, clearly labeled, doesn't cost you anything extra to use it). It's not a replacement for what's being built here, it's just something that already exists and works.

Following along

The VPN page on this site will get updated as things firm up, planned locations, funding model, timeline. If you'd like to help fund the work directly, there's a support link there too, framed honestly: it's a donation toward development, not a purchase of something that exists yet.

Want to see where things stand? The VPN page always reflects the current state, no more and no less than what's actually true.
