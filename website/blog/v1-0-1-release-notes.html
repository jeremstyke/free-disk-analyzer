PurgeCore v1.0.1: Cleanup, Duplicates, RAM, and CSV Export

2026

v1.0.1 adds a Cleanup section and a way to export your scan results, on top of everything in the original v1.0.0 release.

New: Cleanup tab

A new sidebar entry groups four tools:

  • Duplicates - finds files with identical content (1 MB and up), compared by actual content, not just name or size, so renamed copies are caught and same-size-different-content files aren't wrongly flagged.
  • Old Files - the oldest files from your last scan, sorted by last modified date, usually a fast way to spot things you forgot you had.
  • Empty Folders - folders that contain nothing at all, even after checking every subfolder.
  • Free up RAM - asks Windows to reclaim memory that isn't actively in use. Shown with real before/after numbers rather than a promised gain, since the actual effect of this kind of action on modern Windows is often smaller than "RAM cleaner" tools imply.

New: export a scan report to CSV

After a scan on the Analyze tab, an "Export report" button saves a CSV with the scan totals, the storage-by-category breakdown, and the largest folders and files, useful for keeping a record or sharing results.

What stayed the same

Still analysis-only: nothing in this release deletes or modifies any of your files. Still fully local: none of these features send anything about your files anywhere.

Already have v1.0.0 installed? The updated installer will detect it, close the running app, and reinstall over it automatically, no need to uninstall first.
