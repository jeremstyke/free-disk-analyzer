Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes, for good. There's no subscription and no premium tier, ever.

Is my data safe?

Every scan happens on your computer. Free Disk Analyzer never uploads file names, paths, or contents anywhere.

Why does Windows show a warning when I install it?

Free Disk Analyzer doesn't yet have a commercial code-signing certificate, so Windows SmartScreen may flag it as from an unknown publisher. You can verify your download with the SHA-256 checksum published alongside each release.

Can I see the source code?

Not right now, the source isn't public. The compiled app itself is free to use.
