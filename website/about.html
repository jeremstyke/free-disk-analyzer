About Free Disk Analyzer

A small, focused tool built to answer one question: what's actually using your storage.

Why this exists

Most disk space tools either cost money for basic features, or bury the useful information under upsells and bundled "optimizers" of dubious value. Free Disk Analyzer is built the other way around: it does one job (show you what's using your space, clearly and quickly) and it does that job for free, permanently.

Free forever, actually

There's no premium tier, no trial period, no feature held back behind a paywall. Development is funded by a couple of clearly labeled affiliate recommendations (see the Partners section), never by limiting what the free version can do.

Privacy first, by construction

Every scan happens on your own computer. File names, paths, and contents are never uploaded anywhere, there's no server involved in analyzing a disk. See the full Privacy Policy for the specifics of what can and can't be collected.

Who builds this

Free Disk Analyzer is built by Jeremy Jury (jeremstyke), an independent developer who also makes CleanTab, a free privacy-first browser cleaner for Chrome and Edge. Same philosophy across both: free forever, privacy first, no dark patterns.

If Free Disk Analyzer saved you some time (or some disk space), you're welcome to offer a coffee. It's entirely optional and never affects what the app itself costs you: nothing.

Source and contact

The source code is publicly viewable on GitHub. Questions or bug reports are welcome there.
